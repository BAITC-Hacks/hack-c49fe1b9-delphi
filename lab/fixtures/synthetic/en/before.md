# Synthetic policy — Before

Delphi learning fixture. All departments and duties are fictional. This is not a company document or a legal instrument.

## 1. Head of Analytics — position

1.1. Must prepare a consolidated risk report for the whole company every month and submit it to the executive director.

1.2. Must review the completeness of the company-wide risk register annually.

1.3. Must archive the company-wide access-policy exception log in secure storage every quarter.

## 2. Head of Procurement — position

2.1. Has exclusive authority to give final approval to the supplier list for procurement by the northern branch; other departments may not give this approval.

2.2. Concludes procurement contracts for the northern branch with approved suppliers.

## 3. Head of Internal Control — position

3.1. Independently checks compliance with procurement procedures for contracts of the northern branch and does not participate in concluding those contracts.

## 4. Director of the Northern Branch — position

4.1. Prepares a monthly training report covering employees of the northern branch only.

## 5. Director of the Southern Branch — position

5.1. Prepares a monthly training report covering employees of the southern branch only.
